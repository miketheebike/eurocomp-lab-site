Which financial data management tools is the business using? These tools are related to obtaining, storing and reviewing the business financial data. - The business accounting is kept using appropriate software - Invoices and other primary accounting documents are issued and received electronically - Accounting functions are performed using a computer application (processing invoices and payments, generating reports, performing audits, completing/submitting tax forms, etc.) - Obtain information regarding billing, invoice confirmation, payment status - Others - None

Which human resources mangement tools is the business using? These tools are related to streamlining communication and company-employee relations. - Salary calculation using appropriate software - Management of holidays and days off using software connected to the financial accounting system - Online tools that facilitate remote working - Assigning tasks and tracking their fulfillment remotely using a computer system - Track personal development/training and assess well-being and workplace atmosphere using a digital platform - Others - None

Which customer relationship management tools is the business using? These tools are related to obtaining the order history for each customer, collaboration with customers is done using a digital system. - Identification/selection of customers according to specific parameters in the database - Automatic updating of customer data (name, address, etc.) in the IT system - Online purchase of the applicant’s products/services - Estimating sales volumes, analyzing buying habits, organizing discount campaigns, using customer data history analysis software - Customers have online access to transaction status/history - Others - None

Which resources management tools is the business using? These tools are related to the access of data about asset management (raw materials, inventory, financial resources, etc.) from current digital systems. - The inventory of fixed assets is carried out through the scanning function with digital devices - Automating/scheduling orders to prevent resource exhaustion or overstocking in advance using a software application - Automatic calculation of transport and logistics costs (maintenance, fuel costs, routes, hours) - Automatic calculation of the costs of goods/services - Others - None

Which processess digitalization tools is the business using? These tools are related to the digitalization of the processes within the business. - Digital approval/signature (using e.g. electronic signature) of documents, contracts, etc. - Easy interconnection and exploitation of the various digital solutions implemented - Assessment and monitoring of supplier/customer risks through IT systems - Management of joint projects of employees from different departments using IT applications - Others - None

Which security policy tools is the business using? These tools are related to the ability to quickly recover access to data in the event of its loss. - Identifying and analyzing potential threats regarding data alteration or loss; prevention, detection, response and recovery measures - Systematic training and education of employees regarding data security and IT security - Measures against security breaches in telecommuting conditions - Securing communications in the process of transmitting/storing commercial documents/data - Others - None
